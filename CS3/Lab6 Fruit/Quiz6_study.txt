ANSWER KEY AND SHORT EXPLANATIONS
=================================

1.  B - The formula produces weights in the 3-to-8 range; parity selects green or red.
2.  C - The predicate is weight > 5, so only 7 and 6 match at zero-based positions 1 and 3.
3.  B - Advancing prevents find_if() from finding the same matching element repeatedly.
4.  B - The comparator establishes ascending weight order, so max_element finds the greatest.
5.  B - Growth occurs first, and the removal predicate uses apple.weight < minAccept.
6.  A - Unlike map, multimap permits multiple elements with equivalent keys.
7.  C - lower_bound/upper_bound delimit [first orange, first key after all oranges).
8.  A - priority_queue is a max-priority queue here; operator< makes the best quality top().
9.  D - The predicate skips green B, while front_inserter reverses the copied ripe peaches.
10. B - Ripe peaches are erased from basket; remove_if preserves the order of retained greens.
11. B - accumulate() occurs before the program asks how much the apples should grow.
12. A - The lambda changes a copied Apple and returns it to the corresponding output position.
13. B - remove_if() returns the new logical end; erase() removes the leftover physical tail.
14. C - The sorting comparator is left.weight < right.weight.
15. B - rand() % 100 produces 0-99, and adding 1 produces 1-100.
16. A - The implicit enum values are orange=0, pear=1, and apple=2.
17. A - rand() % 30 + 1 produces a nonzero box size from 1 through 30.
18. C - The loop advances two positions before printing, then one more before repeating.
19. B - bind() fixes lighterThan's maxWeight argument at weightToJam, which is 10.0.
20. A - Generated weights are between 3 and 8, so every ripe peach in peck is under 10.
